**First author. Oral presentation.** Directly preliminary to my dissertation work on federated
adaptation.

**My contribution:** I conceived and led this study, adapted GMIC for federated fine-tuning under NVIDIA
FLARE, built the three-site federation and its preprocessing pipeline, implemented and ran all
shared-global and personalized strategies including the module-wise Ditto variant, analyzed the results,
and wrote the manuscript.

**Evidence of venue quality:** 24 submissions, 14 accepted (58%); 7 of the 14 selected for oral
presentation, placing this paper in the top 29% of submissions. 7th edition as a MICCAI satellite event;
proceedings published by Springer.
