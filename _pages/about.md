Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa and graduate research assistant in the Department of Epidemiology at the University of Hawaiʻi Cancer Center, advised by Dr. John A. Shepherd.

I work on machine learning for medical screening, with a focus on whether these systems hold up in the populations they were not developed for. My current research examines where mammographic AI loses performance, for whom, and how to recover it, particularly across populations and institutions and among groups excluded from model development altogether.

Before graduate school I spent eight years in technical leadership roles in the U.S. Army and at UH Mānoa, managing IT operations, networks, and data infrastructure.
